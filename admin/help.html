1. Responsibilities and access

The owner controls the DR Reps catalog, quotes, customer records, order status and seller payouts. A seller is a referral partner who brings customers to DR Reps.

  1. Open the dashboard. Open your DR Reps website and add /admin to the address. Enter the owner password and click Log in.
  2. Use the correct account. The owner password is separate from a customer password and a seller code/password. A seller login does not grant admin access.
  3. Navigate. On a computer, use the left sidebar. On a phone, use the bottom navigation; scroll it sideways to reach additional sections.
  4. Finish safely. Click Log out when finished on a shared device. Use View store to check the customer experience.

Example screens use the repository’s demo records. Use your production store domain and credentials; no live customer data is included.

2. Operate the dashboard on a phone

The same dashboard adapts to a narrow screen. Cards stack vertically and wide tables can scroll horizontally.

  1. Sign in. Open /admin in your phone browser and enter the owner password.
  2. Move between sections. Tap the bottom navigation. Swipe along it for Products, Packages Offers, Marketplace, Referral partners, Commissions, Preview, Settings and Help.
  3. Inspect records. Tap a request/customer/product name to open its detail. Scroll down to the Save button; editing a field alone does not save.
  4. Handle wide data. Swipe inside a wide table to see columns on the right. Keep the request number in view when comparing payment and delivery.
  5. Use desktop when convenient. Bulk Excel entry and comparing many records are easier on a computer. Both devices operate on the same saved store records.

3. Configure contact, payment and commission

Set these before accepting orders. All customer communication and fulfillment belongs to DR Reps.

  1. Contact. Enter the store WhatsApp number with country code, contact email and Instagram handle.
  2. Payment instructions. Under Orders and payment, write the payment methods actually accepted and how customers should confirm payment. Click Save settings.
  3. Seller terms. Set Default commission %, choose Profit or Sale price, and enter Currency. A seller-specific rate can override the default.
  4. Customer discount. Set the discount percentage for seller/referral codes. At 0%, a valid code attributes the order without reducing its price.
  5. Check the result. Open an order as a customer and verify the payment instructions, currency and discount message.

Changing the global commission basis changes calculations on existing won deals too. Reconcile historical payouts before changing Profit to Sale price or back.

4. Edit the home page and FAQ

Use Settings for visible store information; the customer walkthrough is available from the home-page FAQ.

  1. Brand. Edit Store name, Tagline, Home headline, Home intro and the optional Announcement bar.
  2. Campaign slides. Upload landscape images, add each small heading/title/link, and save. Empty slots use the site’s fallback campaign.
  3. FAQ text. Write each question as a Q: line followed by its A: line. Save settings, then open the home page and scroll to FAQ.
  4. Customer instructions. The FAQ includes a Website guide entry linking to the real UI walkthrough and its PDF. Keep factual answers consistent with current store terms.
  5. Verify devices. Check the home page on a computer and phone. Confirm every campaign link and the guide download opens correctly.

5. Create and maintain products

Products are DR Reps catalog listings. They have no public selling price; the price is quoted per request.

  1. Create. Open Products and click New product. Add Name, Brand, Category, Condition, Sizes and Description.
  2. Sizes. Enter sizes separated by commas, for example 40, 41, 42 or S, M, L. Leave empty for products without sizes.
  3. Photos. Upload product photos and wait for processing. Review the result before publishing.
  4. Publish or feature. Click Publish product. In an existing record, select Feature on the home page if appropriate, then Save changes.
  5. Hide or delete. Hide from the store preserves the product record. Delete product removes it; existing requests retain their saved item details.

A listing does not reserve stock or guarantee a size. Verify supplier availability, condition and quantity when preparing a quote.

6. Use the product photo tool

The photo tool works inside the product editor. It keeps the original photo and places its cut-out on a backdrop.

  1. Upload. Click Drop photos here or click to upload on a computer, or tap it on a phone to choose an image.
  2. Wait. With Auto clean-up on, wait for background removal and upload. Keep the editor open until the status finishes.
  3. Review. Inspect edges, colour, logos and product details. Select the original if clean-up is unsuitable; re-clean or change backdrop using the photo controls.
  4. AI scenes. In Settings, generate an empty AI scene if a provider is connected. Select the saved scene in the product editor. The product cut-out is placed over it.
  5. Save. Processing a photo does not publish the product. Click Publish product or Save changes after reviewing all details.

Supported uploads: JPG, PNG, WEBP or GIF. Owner uploads are limited to 15 MB. If processing fails, keep the original or retry a smaller file.

7. Bulk add products from a spreadsheet

Bulk add from Excel creates new products from tab-separated rows. It does not update or deduplicate existing products.

  1. Prepare columns. Use this order: Name, Brand, Category, Condition, Sizes, Description, Image link. Only Name is required.
  2. Copy. Select the rows in Excel or Google Sheets and copy them. Keep one product per row and comma-separated sizes in one cell.
  3. Paste. Open Products, expand Bulk add from Excel and paste the rows into the box.
  4. Import. Click Import once. A header named Name or Product is skipped. Check the imported count and open representative products.
  5. Avoid duplicates. Search the catalog before repeating an import. Correct an existing item through its editor rather than importing it again.

8. Create a Packages Offer

Packages Offers are independent fixed-price bundles. Their records do not reuse the main Products catalog.

  1. Open the tab. Open Packages Offers in the owner sidebar or phone navigation, then New package.
  2. Describe the bundle. Upload an image, enter a name and positive fixed price in the configured store currency. In Included items and quantities, state exactly what one package contains.
  3. Set options and terms. Enter comma-separated sizes/options if needed. Explain any mixed-size requirements and delivery terms in Description and terms.
  4. Publish. Choose an Availability report. Keep Published checked to list it on the home page and /packages. Click Save package.
  5. Verify. Open the customer offer on phone and computer. Check the included quantities, one-package price, options and stock notice.

Example prices in these screenshots are sample data. A bundle request records the displayed price and contents at submission; no stock is reserved.

9. Recheck packages every 48 hours

Every public offer displays Updates every 48 hours and explains that stock is not guaranteed. The owner must carry out the actual review.

  1. Check suppliers. At least every 48 hours, confirm the actual bundle items, sizes, quantities and current price with the supplier.
  2. Update intentionally. Open the package, correct its contents/price/options, choose the latest Availability report and check I checked stock now.
  3. Save. Click Save package. The checkbox refreshes Last checked. Editing text without it keeps the previous stock-check time.
  4. Unavailable or withdrawn. Choose Unavailable to block new stock requests while retaining the listing, or clear Published to remove it from the customer site.
  5. Review stale offers. After 48 hours without a recorded check, the offer shows Needs stock recheck. Recheck it before accepting payment.

The notice is a review policy, not an automated stock feed or a stock guarantee. Even Reported available requires confirmation for the customer’s request.

10. Fulfill a package request

Package requests join the normal owner queue and the customer’s My orders. They start with the bundle amount and no price-unlock code.

  1. Inspect the snapshot. Open the request. Check Package: name, quantities, chosen option, included-item note and customer’s city/notes.
  2. Confirm stock and charges. Check every included item and quantity. Confirm delivery charges and any requested mixed sizes with the customer before accepting money.
  3. Finalize the quote. Enter the final whole-request Price before discount, supplier cost and clear quote terms. Click Save to generate the price code.
  4. Send and follow. Send the current code through WhatsApp. Follow customer unlocking, any valid referral discount, payment and delivery using the ordinary order process.
  5. If stock fails. Agree an alternative or mark Lost. Editing today’s package does not rewrite an earlier request’s saved item/offer snapshot.

A package can be referred by a partner and use the configured discount. Marketplace shop enquiries are separate and do not enter this request/commission lifecycle.

11. Enable or disable Sellers and Distributors

Both marketplace sections start off on production. The admin tab and saved records remain available so you can prepare listings for a future launch.

  1. Open Marketplace. Use Marketplace in the owner sidebar or phone tabs.
  2. Choose Sellers. Check Enable Marketplace Sellers to display independent retail businesses and their own products.
  3. Choose Distributors. Check Enable Distributors to display independent distributors in any product category. It does not depend on the Sellers switch.
  4. Save visibility. Click Save visibility. Enabled sections show their customer menu, home section, public pages and application option.
  5. Disable later. Clear either switch and save to hide that type from customers. Its records remain editable in admin. Pausing one business hides only that business.

Marketplace listings are separate from DR Reps clothing/catalog items, Packages Offers and hidden referral partners. Neither switch creates a merchant login, shared checkout or payment split.

12. Approve and manage marketplace shops

Marketplace Sellers and Distributors are independent businesses in any product category, with their own pages and products. They are separate from Referral partners.

  1. Review applications. Open Marketplace in the admin panel. A List your shop application creates a pending record that stays private.
  2. Verify the shop. Open its record, verify identity/contact details and agree the listing terms with the merchant. Add an image, description, city and product category and listing type (Seller or Distributor).
  3. Publish. Set Publication status to published and click Save shop. The business appears in its enabled customer section and on the home page. If its section is disabled, it stays private even when marked published.
  4. Add their products. After saving the shop, use Add shop product. Enter name, optional price, sizes, description and image; keep Visible product checked and save.
  5. Update or pause. Use Save product for edits or clear Visible product. Set the shop to paused/rejected/pending to hide its entire public page. Test View public shop after changes.

This version lists stores and supports direct contact with them. It does not create merchant logins, merchant checkout, marketplace payment splitting or referral commission on direct shop sales.

13. Create a seller / referral partner

Create a private seller account so the partner can share links and track commission. Customers continue shopping with DR Reps.

  1. Add the partner. Open Referral partners and click New seller. Enter name, phone, optional email and city.
  2. Choose a code. Enter a unique code such as AHMED01, or leave it blank to generate one. Codes accept letters, numbers and hyphens.
  3. Set terms. Set Commission % or leave it blank for the default. Keep Active selected and click Create seller.
  4. Deliver login details. The generated password appears once. Copy the invite or use Send login on WhatsApp, then actually send it in WhatsApp.
  5. Check the referral. Open the customer link: it should open the DR Reps shop with no personal seller storefront. Use Preview to check the partner’s private panel.

The partner has no product ownership, supplier control, pricing permission, payment authority or fulfillment role in this website.

14. Pause, reset or remove a seller

Open Referral partners, then click the partner’s name. The code is fixed after creation.

  1. Edit. Change contact details, private bio, commission rate or Active, then Save changes.
  2. Pause. Clear Active and save. The partner cannot sign in and new referral links/codes are no longer accepted as active.
  3. Reset access. Click New password. Copy the one-time password and deliver it securely. The old password and old sessions stop working.
  4. Review existing orders. Pausing does not erase past requests or automatically clear saved customer referrals. Inspect existing attribution and amounts explicitly.
  5. Delete only when intended. Delete seller removes the login record. Past requests keep its code. Settle/reconcile owed commission before deletion.

Do not reuse a deleted code for a different person: old requests and saved customer referrals still carry that code.

15. Review the request pipeline

Overview summarizes activity. Requests is the working queue for each order or seller-logged enquiry.

  1. Find work. Click Requests or Open inbox. Filter by status/seller or search for a name, phone or item.
  2. Open the record. Click a request number/name. Review items, size, quantity, customer notes and source.
  3. Start sourcing. Choose Searching, enter supplier information and add an internal note. Click Save.
  4. Keep the timeline current. Record calls, alternatives and promised dates in Add a note. These notes belong to the internal timeline. Click Save to record the note.
  5. Close correctly. Quoted means a quote exists; Won means the deal is counted as successful; Lost means it will not proceed. Payment and delivery are separate fields.

16. Set the quote and price

Enter the total quoted selling price for this request, not a catalog price or an unlabelled per-piece price.

  1. Check attribution. Review Seller. Keep the correct referral code or select No seller. Set Commission % if this deal has a special agreed rate.
  2. Write the quote. Describe the agreed items, sizes, quantities, condition, delivery timing and any included charges in Quote for the customer.
  3. Enter amounts. Enter Price before discount and Supplier cost. Use the same currency and whole-order basis for both.
  4. Check the calculation. Read Customer pays, Profit, Seller commission and You keep. Discounts reduce the customer amount before commission is calculated.
  5. Save. Click Save. A new price code is created when the price is first entered, and New/Searching normally moves to Quoted.

For profit-based commission, a missing supplier cost is treated as zero in the calculation. Enter the real cost before closing a deal.

17. Send a price code and handle repricing

The price code unlocks one order’s price. It is separate from a seller/referral discount code.

  1. Find the code. After saving a price, use the Price code card. Click Send code on WhatsApp or Copy message.
  2. Send the message. WhatsApp opens a prepared message. Review the recipient, request number and code, then press Send.
  3. Check unlocking. The card shows whether the customer has unlocked the quote. A wrong or outdated code is rejected.
  4. Reprice before payment. Before the order is marked Paid, change Price and Save. A new code replaces the old one and the customer must unlock the updated price again.
  5. Explain the change. Send the new code and clarify the revised terms. Existing discount percentage remains on the request, so verify the new customer total.

Saving a price or opening WhatsApp does not prove the customer received a message. Send it and confirm the correct order link.

18. Confirm payment and update delivery

Payment records customer money. Commission paid records money paid to the referral partner. They are different controls.

  1. Confirm receipt. After verifying the agreed payment with the customer, set Payment to Paid and Save.
  2. Check status. Marking Paid moves the request to Won. Review the commission rate and amounts before saving; correct a mistaken payment status explicitly.
  3. Track fulfillment. Set Delivery to Preparing, then On the way, then Delivered as those events actually happen. Save each update.
  4. Use separate payout control. Leave Commission paid to the seller unchecked until the partner has actually been paid.
  5. Keep customer informed. Customers see Payment and Delivery on their order page. Send tracking details or delivery changes through the store’s contact channel.

There is no built-in payment gateway or courier tracking integration. The owner verifies payment and records delivery updates manually.

19. Maintain customers and reset their access

CRM records are matched by the full WhatsApp number including country code. An Account badge indicates a customer login exists.

  1. Find the customer. Open Customers. Search by name, phone, email or city, or filter by a tag.
  2. Review history. Open the name to see requests, spend and saved Seller/referral. Check the specific request as well as the customer-level referral.
  3. Edit details. Update name, email, city, comma-separated tags, private notes or the saved seller, then Save. Phone is read-only in this screen.
  4. Reset an account password. Use New password for an account holder. The generated password is shown once; deliver it to the verified customer. Resetting signs out their sessions.
  5. Export. Use Export CSV from Customers when a spreadsheet is needed. Store the file privately because it includes customer contact information.

Seller-created requests appear in CRM but do not automatically appear in the customer’s My orders account. A seller-entered enquiry cannot change an existing account’s profile or saved referral.

20. Understand commission calculations

Commission is counted on requests marked Won with Payment = Paid, a sale price and a stored commission rate.

  1. Customer total. Quoted price × (100 − discount %) ÷ 100.
  2. Profit basis. Commission = max(0, customer total − supplier cost) × commission %.
  3. Sale basis. Commission = customer total × commission %. Supplier cost does not change this basis.
  4. Rate order. A rate entered for the deal takes priority. When a deal becomes Won without a rate, the app uses the seller rate or default and stores it on the request.
  5. Example. Price 1,000; discount 5%; cost 600; rate 10% of profit. Customer pays 950; profit is 350; seller earns 35; store keeps 315 after cost and commission.

The example is arithmetic only. Rates, currency and payout timing depend on the owner’s configured terms. Negative profit produces zero commission.

21. Pay commission and correct payout marks

The dashboard records payout status; it does not transfer money to a seller.

  1. Review owed amounts. Open Commissions. Unpaid shows eligible paid, won deals awaiting payout; All won deals also shows paid entries.
  2. Reconcile. Check each deal’s discounted sale, cost, basis, rate and commission against the agreed terms.
  3. Pay outside the app. Use the agreed payment method and retain proof. Only then click Mark paid on the corresponding deal.
  4. Settle a whole partner. Mark all paid marks all currently unpaid eligible requests for that partner. Review the group before confirming.
  5. Correct a mistaken mark. Under All won deals, click Undo paid for the affected deal. This changes the record only; it does not reverse a real transfer. For a refunded deal, open its request from the reconciliation notice.

Paid to you uses the recorded payout amount, which stays fixed when a deal is later refunded. The flags are not bank confirmations; reconcile actual transfers separately.

22. Unavailable, cancellation, refund and corrections

These cases need explicit owner handling; the app does not automate returns or financial reversals.

Unavailable item / declined quote

Agree alternatives with the customer, add an internal note and mark Lost if the request will not proceed.

Customer changes item, size or quantity

Confirm the amendment in the quote/note. There is no customer self-edit of a submitted order; a new request is often clearest.

Refund after payment

Record Payment = Refunded, explain the amount and method in notes, and perform the refund outside the app.

Refunded deal and commission

Marking Refunded moves the request to Refunded and removes it from earned and owed commission. If a partner payout was marked paid, Commissions flags it for reconciliation; review the actual transfer and then update the request payout flag.

Partial refund / partial delivery

There are no dedicated partial-refund or partial-shipment fields. Keep agreed amounts and fulfillment details in notes; reconcile manually.

Mistaken Won or Paid

Correct Payment deliberately and save. Changing Paid back to Not paid removes Won status. If a seller payout was marked paid, clear that record first before changing attribution, cost or rate; reconcile any actual transfer separately.

23. Referral and attribution scenarios

Attribution belongs to an order. The customer’s saved referral is a fallback for future requests.

Customer opens /s/CODE

Active code sets a 30-day browser referral cookie, then redirects to the DR Reps shop without a public seller identity.

Customer opens product?ref=CODE

An active code is recorded silently while the customer remains on the DR Reps product page.

Returning customer / cookie expired

The saved customer referral can still be used when there is no new valid code.

Another valid code is supplied

The new valid code can credit that order. The first saved customer referral remains unless the owner explicitly changes the customer record.

Discount code is applied

The active code replaces that order’s referral and applies the configured discount after unlocking and before payment.

Inactive/deleted/invalid code

New link/coupon validation requires an active seller. Old saved referrals and historical records are not automatically erased; review exceptions manually.

24. Owner troubleshooting

Cannot log in

Check the owner password; repeated wrong attempts trigger a 15-minute wait. Missing login configuration requires the maintainer to set the secrets.

Request exists but WhatsApp was not sent

Ask the customer to use Open WhatsApp again on the order page. Do not create another request just to resend the message.

Price code fails

Check the request number and latest saved code. Eight code attempts in an hour trigger a wait; send the current code.

Email does not arrive

Check Settings → Connections, email address and request timeline. Use Send test email. Credentials/Zoho setup are maintained separately.

Photo upload fails

Check format and size, retry a smaller file, review R2/AI connection status, or keep the original photo.

Totals change unexpectedly

Inspect discount, supplier cost, deal rate, Status, Payment and global commission basis. Seller totals use all attributed requests; the visible request list shows the latest 200, and the commission list is capped at 1,000 deals.

25. Daily handover checklist

Use this sequence at the beginning and end of each operating day.

  1. Open work. Review New, Searching and Quoted requests. Confirm who is waiting for a price or a reply.
  2. Update promises. Record sourcing progress, quote changes and delivery timing in the appropriate request.
  3. Reconcile money. Verify customer payments separately from seller payouts. Record refunds and corrections explicitly.
  4. Verify the store. Check the customer FAQ guide, seller Help and admin Help. Test the links and review any changed products or settings.
  5. Hand over. Give the next admin outstanding request numbers, promises, unresolved refunds and payout proof. Provide the correct store domain and owner access securely.

Deployment, database/media backup and secret maintenance are described in the private repository SETUP.md. This guide covers operating the website; it does not certify a production deployment.
