What are Packages Offers?

Fixed-price bundles listed separately from the main catalog. Updates every 48 hours. Stock is not guaranteed: request stock confirmation before purchasing.

Browse Packages Offers

Who are Marketplace Sellers and Distributors?

Independent businesses in any product category listed on DR Reps. Contact the shop directly about its products, payment, delivery and returns. Referral partners are separate: they only introduce customers to DR Reps.

Marketplace SellersDistributors

Why don't you show prices?

Our stock comes from partner companies clearing deadstock and surplus, so nothing sits in a warehouse with a fixed price. When you request an item we check our network and quote you the best price we can find.

Do I pay anything to send a request?

No. Requests are free and there's nothing to pay online. We contact you with a price first, and you decide.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within one to two working days, on WhatsApp or by phone.

Can you find something that isn't listed?

Very often, yes. Use "Request anything" and describe the item, size and quantity.

Drip stays.. Worry leaves..

Deadstock and discounted stock from partner companies. Every price is quoted to you personally, on request.
