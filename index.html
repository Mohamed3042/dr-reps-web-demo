Questions

Why don't you show prices?

Our stock comes from partner companies clearing deadstock and surplus, so nothing sits in a warehouse with a fixed price. When you request an item we check our network and quote you the best price we can find.

Do I pay anything to send a request?

No. Requests are free and there's nothing to pay online. We contact you with a price first, and you decide.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within one to two working days, on WhatsApp or by phone.

Can you find something that isn't listed?

Very often, yes. Use "Request anything" and describe the item, size and quantity.
